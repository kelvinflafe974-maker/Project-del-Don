## Filming
- Morning light from a window, around 7 to 8am. A made bed or bedside table. Nothing branded except the shirt.
- Phone back camera, vertical, 1080x1920 or 4K, 30fps. Lock exposure on the shirt so the orange bar doesn't blow out.
- Keep every card inside the safe zone: nothing in the top 230px, the bottom 480px or the right 230px.
- Record the voice to camera, or as a voiceover over the close-ups. Burn in captions, since most people watch muted first.
- If you add music, keep it quiet under the voice, or skip it. Say "link", not "shirt", in the call to action.
