## 2. What the research says (read the caveat)

Instagram blocks anonymous viewing, so per the skill rules I used public YouTube Shorts instead: 47 Shorts from 10 faith-apparel and adjacent channels, each ranked against its own channel median. Full table in `swipe.md`.

What sat in the top third:
- **Specific product plus named verse** beat generic "faith in style" posts on the same accounts (I'm With Jesus: "Galatians 2:20 Christian T-Shirt" at 34x its median).
- **A founder turn**: "When a Marvel artist stops working for the publishers and starts creating for the Lord" (Found One Apparel, 7x).
- **A firm position said plainly**: "Why I'm NOT AFRAID of What's Coming" (DLM, 65x), "Eve was Adam's rib not the other way around" (Rianna Siobhan, 9x).
- **A warning story**: "Brand owners beware" (Mr. BeSpecial, 82x). Only usable if you have a true one.

What sat in the bottom third: announcements. "New samples are in", "How we set up at events", "One-day sale", "Keep going".

**Confidence: low.** Most of these channels get hundreds of views, 41 of 47 hooks came from titles because the Shorts had no captions, and the classifier could not name most of them. It points a direction; it proves nothing. Rerun it with your own Instagram Saved folder and 6 to 10 accounts you choose.

How it shaped the scripts: every reel names one design and its verse, three reels take a position, and the event post is written as an invitation with a reason, not an announcement.

- **Humaniser:** every script landed between 54 and 72, rated REVIEW, not PASS. The weak signal is specificity. That is because I would not invent numbers or customers. Read each one out loud and swap any line you would not say.
- **Hook length:** four spoken hooks run about 3.6 seconds against a 3 second target. The 8-word versions scored 50 to 63, so I kept the stronger line and put the short version on the first card, which is what people read at frame 1.
- **Check scripture wording:** check every quoted verse against the Bible version you use before filming. The site uses NKJV.
