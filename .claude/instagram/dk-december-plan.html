ig-viral · low confidence

What the research says

Instagram blocks anonymous viewing, so I used public YouTube Shorts: 47 Shorts from 10 faith-apparel and adjacent channels. Each was ranked by how far it beat its own channel's usual views, not by raw views.

Top third

  • One named product with its verse. "Galatians 2:20 Christian T-Shirt" ran at 34x its channel's median.
  • A founder's turn. "When a Marvel artist stops working for the publishers and starts creating for the Lord", 7x.
  • A position said plainly. "Why I'm NOT AFRAID of What's Coming", 65x.

Bottom third

  • Announcements: "New samples are in", "How we set up at events", "One-day sale", "Keep going".

So the event reel is an invitation with a reason, not an announcement.

Why low confidence

Most of these channels get hundreds of views. 41 of the 47 hooks are video titles, because the Shorts had no captions. It points a direction and proves nothing. Rerun it with your own Instagram Saved folder.
