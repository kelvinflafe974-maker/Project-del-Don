## My positions

1. You do not have to dress boringly to prove you are a Christian. Christian fashion can be contemporary, fashionable and expressive.
2. Being fashionable and being spiritually grounded are not mutually exclusive. Appearance and faith can coexist.
3. Wearing Christian clothing does not make you a committed Christian. Your lifestyle should reflect the message on your shirt.
4. Christian brands should compete on quality and creativity, not lean on the fact that they sell Christian products. Being a Christian business is not an excuse for poor products.
5. Faith should be visible outside church. It should shape how you live every day, not only how you behave on Sunday.

These should start real conversations, not manufactured controversy.
